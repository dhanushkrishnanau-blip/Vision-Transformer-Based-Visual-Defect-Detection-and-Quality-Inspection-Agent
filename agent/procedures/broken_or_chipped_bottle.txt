BROKEN OR CHIPPED BOTTLE — CORRECTIVE ACTION

What to check: Hold the bottle and inspect the rim, neck, base, and sidewalls under good light. Look for cracks, chips, sharp edges, and loose glass. Do not handle a visibly broken bottle without the site's required protective equipment.

Who to notify: Notify the line supervisor and quality-control lead. Follow the site's glass-breakage and product-containment procedure if fragments are present.

Hold or release: Keep the affected bottle and the identified production interval on hold. Release only after an authorized quality reviewer documents that the product is safe and conforms to specifications.
