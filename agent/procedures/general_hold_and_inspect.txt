GENERAL HOLD AND INSPECT — CORRECTIVE ACTION

What to check: Segregate the flagged item, inspect it under suitable lighting, and compare it with the approved specification. Record the image and the physical findings.

Who to notify: Notify the line supervisor and quality-control lead. Escalate safety concerns through the site's established procedure.

Hold or release: Keep the item on hold until an authorized reviewer decides whether it meets requirements. Do not release based only on an automated score.
