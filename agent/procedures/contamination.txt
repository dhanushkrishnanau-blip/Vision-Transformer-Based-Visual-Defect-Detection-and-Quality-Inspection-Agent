CONTAMINATION — CORRECTIVE ACTION

What to check: Hold the product and inspect the inside and outside for residue, foreign material, stains, fibers, and cleanliness concerns. Do not taste, wipe, or otherwise alter the evidence.

Who to notify: Notify the line supervisor and quality-control lead. Contact the site's food-safety or environmental-health contact when the product or process requires it.

Hold or release: Keep affected product and the relevant production interval on hold while the source and extent are investigated. Release only with documented approval from the authorized quality or safety reviewer.
