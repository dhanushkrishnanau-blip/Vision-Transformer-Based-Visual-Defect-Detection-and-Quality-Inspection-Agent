UNSPECIFIED ANOMALY — CORRECTIVE ACTION

What to check: Keep the item available for inspection and compare its shape, surface, finish, and markings with an approved reference sample. Record the observed location without assuming the defect type.

Who to notify: Notify the line supervisor and quality-control lead for a manual assessment.

Hold or release: Hold the item and any potentially related production until a qualified reviewer identifies the issue. Release only after the reviewer records an acceptable inspection result.
